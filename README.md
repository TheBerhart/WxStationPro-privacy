# Privacy Policy for Wx Station Pro

**Effective Date:** [Aug 7, 2026]  
**Last Updated:** [Aug 7, 2026]

Wx Station Pro (“we,” “our,” or “us”) provides weather forecasts and airport information to the general public and aviation users. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

## Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of information:

- **Location Data**: When you allow location access, we collect your device’s approximate or precise location to show relevant local weather and nearby airports. We request “While Using the App” permission whenever possible.
- **Usage Data**: Information about how you interact with the app (features used, screens viewed, crashes).
- **Device Information**: Device type, operating system version, and unique device identifiers needed for app functionality and diagnostics.
- **Optional Account or Feedback Data**: If you contact us or create an account (if offered), we may collect your name, email address, or the content of your messages.

We do **not** sell your personal information.

## How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

- Provide accurate weather forecasts and airport information based on your location or searched locations.
- Improve app performance, reliability, and features.
- Diagnose and fix crashes or technical issues.
- Respond to your support requests or feedback.
- Comply with legal obligations.

## Third-Party Services

We may use third-party services to help operate the app, such as:

- Weather and aviation data providers
- Analytics or crash-reporting tools (if used)
- Cloud hosting or content delivery services

These providers only receive the data necessary to perform their services and are required to protect it in accordance with this policy and applicable law.

## Data Retention

We retain information only as long as needed to provide the service, improve the app, or comply with legal requirements. Location data used for current weather/airport display is generally processed in real time and not stored long-term in identifiable form.

## Your Choices and Rights

- You can control location access at any time in your device Settings.
- You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data by contacting us at the email below.
- You can stop using the app at any time.

If you are in a region with additional privacy rights (such as the European Economic Area, California, or similar), you may have further rights regarding your data. Contact us to exercise them.

## Children’s Privacy

Wx Station Pro is not directed at children under 13 (or the applicable age in your region). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
